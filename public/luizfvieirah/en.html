About

I started in the back end of a fintech: 2 years and 8 months at Sinqia, a Brazilian maker of financial and accounting systems, working with Java and SQL Server in production on stored procedures, indexes, query tuning and incidents resolved directly with clients. That is where I unblocked a credit pipeline stalled by a deadlock and sped up a delinquency report that used to take minutes.

In 2022 I chose to become full-stack: I took freelance work in TypeScript and Next.js and, in 2026, launched FolioBy, a portfolio SaaS with recurring subscriptions. Now I am returning to Java back-end work in the financial domain with WorkFlow Crédito, a credit pipeline in Java 21 and Spring Boot 3.

I am looking for a Java back-end or full-stack role, remote, hybrid or on-site. I speak Portuguese (native) and fluent English, plus conversational Spanish and Italian.
