About

I am a developer with a foundation in Java and SQL, built over 2 years and 8 months at Sinqia, a Brazilian fintech that makes financial and accounting systems. There I worked in production with SQL Server, stored procedures, indexes and query tuning, and resolved incidents end to end directly with clients.

Since 2022 I have worked as a full-stack developer with TypeScript and Next.js. I am now building WorkFlow Crédito, a credit-pipeline back end in Java 21 and Spring Boot 3, with hexagonal architecture, TDD and Testcontainers.

I hold a degree in Mathematics from UFPR and a technical diploma in Computing from IFPR. I speak Portuguese, English, Spanish and Italian. I am looking for a Java back-end or full-stack role, remote, hybrid or on-site, and I am comfortable working across time zones.
